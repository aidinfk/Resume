PROFILE

Summary

Computer Science researcher working at the intersection of Process Mining, AI, and sustainable computing, with a focus on healthcare applications.

First author of a published systematic review on healthcare process mining, a submitted review proposing a polyglot hybrid database architecture to improve analytical performance while reducing energy and CO₂ footprint, and an in-progress review on Agentic AI in software engineering.

Also a contributing author on peer-reviewed research in software defect prediction and metaheuristic optimization. Proficient in Python, SQL, and a range of database and machine-learning tools.

Holds M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Software/Computer Engineering from Islamic Azad University, Tabriz Branch.

RESEARCH WORK

Academic Experience

Academic Research

Research Assistant

Islamic Azad University, Tabriz

2023–2025
  • Conducted a PRISMA 2020-based systematic review of Process Mining in healthcare, analyzing algorithmic trends, tool use, and clinical applications including pathway analysis, conformance checking, and bottleneck detection. Published in the International Journal of Data Science and Analytics, Springer. First author.
  • Designed and evaluated a three-tier polyglot persistence architecture using MySQL, MongoDB, and Redis for healthcare process mining, reducing execution time by approximately 18.6% and energy consumption by approximately 14.2% while preserving predictive model quality (F₁-score = 0.82 ± 0.02).
Independent Research

Independent Research Collaboration

With Faculty Advisors, Islamic Azad University, Tabriz

2025–Present
  • Expanded the M.Sc. thesis into a full manuscript, “Toward Sustainable Healthcare Process Mining: A Polyglot Hybrid Database Architecture for Efficient Analytics and CO₂ Reduction,” submitted to the International Journal of Sustainable Computing: Informatics and Systems, Elsevier. First author.
  • Co-authored a systematic review of advanced learning techniques for software defect prediction, covering ensemble learning, deep learning, GNNs, and Transformer-based models based on 32 primary studies. Currently under peer review.
