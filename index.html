RESEARCH PROFILE

Summary

Computer Science researcher with an academic background in Computer Engineering and a research focus on Process Mining, data-intensive systems, sustainable data architectures, and AI/ML methods for software engineering and data analysis.

My research trajectory developed from software development and system design toward data analytics, Process Mining, data-driven system architecture, and the application of AI methods to real-world problems.

I am particularly interested in healthcare Process Mining, hybrid and polyglot architectures for large and heterogeneous data, computational efficiency and sustainability, and Agentic AI for smarter software engineering and data-driven systems.

My research experience includes systematic literature reviews, experimental evaluation, scientific writing, data analysis, Process Mining, and the design of software and database architectures.
